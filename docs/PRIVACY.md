# Right Click Office to PDF · 隱私權政策 / Privacy policy

發行者 / Publisher — Yu-Han · 2026-11-24

## 繁體中文

Right Click Office to PDF 使用電腦上的 Microsoft Word、PowerPoint 或 Excel 轉換你選取的文件。工具會在本機處理選取的檔案路徑、文件內容、輸出位置及轉檔結果，並將 PDF 儲存在來源文件旁。批次佇列與轉檔過程會使用本機暫存檔。你可以自行管理或刪除產生的 PDF。

本工具不要求註冊帳號，不含廣告或使用情況追蹤，也不會將文件、檔案路徑或轉檔結果傳送給開發者。Microsoft Store、Microsoft Office 以及你選用的同步資料夾（例如 OneDrive）會依各自的設定及隱私權政策運作。

如有關於本工具的隱私權問題，可透過[專案支援頁面](https://github.com/kaoshou/right-click-office-to-pdf/issues)聯絡發行者。該頁面公開顯示留言，請避免附上私人文件、完整檔案路徑或其他個人資料。

## English

Right Click Office to PDF uses Microsoft Word, PowerPoint, or Excel on your computer to convert the documents you select. The app processes selected file paths, document contents, output locations, and conversion results locally. It saves PDFs next to the source documents and uses local temporary files for the batch queue and conversion. You control the generated PDFs and can delete them at any time.

The app requires no account, contains no advertising or usage tracking, and does not send your documents, file paths, or conversion results to the developer. Microsoft Store, Microsoft Office, and any synced folders you use (such as OneDrive) operate under their own settings and privacy policies.

For privacy questions, contact the publisher through the [project support page](https://github.com/kaoshou/right-click-office-to-pdf/issues). Messages there are public. Please omit private documents, full file paths, and other personal information.
